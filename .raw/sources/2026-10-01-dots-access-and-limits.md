# Dots access, plans and limits

Verified date: 2026-10-01
Source publication dates: 2026-09-29 (launch); help pages undated
Method: official documentation reviewed; short original summary

Pro 100/200/500 eligible for users 18+ outside the EEA, UK and Switzerland; Business Premium and Enterprise worldwide, Enterprise off by default. Rollout is gradual.
First dot included in plan. Dot conversations do not count toward ChatGPT limits; Work and Codex tasks the dot starts do. Extended limits for the first month; per-plan usage terms to be announced. Numeric allowances undisclosed.
Model: GPT-6 Astra. Create a dot on desktop app or desktop browser only. Channels: ChatGPT, user-started voice calls, Slack, Teams; texting is a US-only Pro beta.
