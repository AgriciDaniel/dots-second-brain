# Dot Access and Plans

As of the 2026-09-29 launch, dots are available on Pro plans for users 18 and older outside the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and on Business Premium and Enterprise worldwide (Enterprise off by default). Rollout is gradual. The first dot is included in the plan.

- Dot conversations do not count toward ChatGPT limits; Work and Codex tasks the dot starts do.
- Numeric allowances were not disclosed at launch; extended limits apply for the first month.
- A dot is created from the desktop app or a desktop browser. Channels: ChatGPT, user-started voice calls, Slack and Teams; texting is a US-only Pro beta.

> [!stale]
> Plans, regions and limits change quickly. Recheck [[Dots Access, Plans and Limits]] before relying on them.
